LEANDRÉ'S UGC WEBSITE — SETUP (about 30 minutes, all free)
==========================================================

What's in this folder
  index.html     the website itself (you never need to edit it)
  content/       all your words, prices, photos and videos (edited from the dashboard)
  admin/         your editing dashboard (yoursite/admin)
  assets/uploads where your uploaded photos and videos are stored

------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 — Make a GitHub account (this is where your site files live)
------------------------------------------------------------
Done: your username (leandreeugc) is already filled in.

------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 — Upload the files
------------------------------------------------------------
1. On GitHub click the + (top right) > New repository.
2. Name it:  leandre-ugc
   Choose Public, tick "Add a README file", click Create repository.
3. Click "Add file" > "Upload files".
4. Open this folder on your computer, select EVERYTHING inside it
   (index.html, admin, content, assets, this file) and drag it into the page.
   Drag the contents, not the folder itself.
5. Click "Commit changes".

------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3 — Put the site online with Netlify
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Go to netlify.com > Sign up > "Sign up with GitHub".
2. Click "Add new project" (or "Add new site") > "Import an existing project" > GitHub.
3. Pick  leandre-ugc . Leave the build settings empty. Click Deploy.
4. When it's done you'll get an address like  random-name-123.netlify.app
   To change it: Project configuration > General > Change project name > leandre-ugc
   Your site is now at  https://leandre-ugc.netlify.app  (or whatever name you chose)

------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4 — Turn on your dashboard login
------------------------------------------------------------
A) On GitHub:
   1. Click your profile photo > Settings > Developer settings (bottom left) > OAuth Apps > New OAuth App.
   2. Fill in:
        Application name:            Leandre site editor
        Homepage URL:                https://leandre-ugc.netlify.app   (your site address)
        Authorization callback URL:  https://api.netlify.com/auth/done
   3. Click Register application.
   4. Copy the Client ID. Click "Generate a new client secret" and copy that too.

B) On Netlify (your site):
   1. Project configuration > Access & security > OAuth > Install provider.
   2. Choose GitHub, paste the Client ID and Client secret, click Install.

------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5 — Edit your site any time
------------------------------------------------------------
Go to  https://leandre-ugc.netlify.app/admin  > Login with GitHub.

  • Videos / Photography: add, remove, drag to reorder, upload files
  • Packages & add-ons: change prices, add or delete packages
  • Site settings: colours, your email, social links, the "Work with me" link,
    and the page layout (drag sections to reorder, untick to hide)
  • Put *stars* around words in a heading to make them pink and italic

Click Publish after editing. Your live site updates in about a minute.

------------------------------------------------------------
TIPS FOR FULL QUALITY
------------------------------------------------------------
  • Videos: MP4, ideally under 25 MB each (1080p, 30–60s is usually fine).
    iPhone: Settings > Camera > Formats > Most Compatible, so videos save as MP4-friendly files.
  • Photos: full-size JPG or PNG straight from your camera/editing app.
    Avoid screenshots or files sent through WhatsApp/Instagram (they get compressed).
  • Files are stored exactly as you upload them; nothing is compressed.

OPTIONAL — your own address (e.g. leandreugc.com)
  Buy a domain (about £10/year) and add it in Netlify: Domain management > Add a domain.
